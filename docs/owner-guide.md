# Gym Admin: Owner's Guide

A short guide for daily use. Everything works on your phone.

## Install it on your phone (once)
- **Android (Chrome):** open the website → menu ⋮ → **Install app**.
- **iPhone (Safari):** open the website → Share button → **Add to Home Screen**.

It then opens like a normal app from your home screen.

## Every morning (1–2 minutes)
1. Open the app. The dashboard shows **"N reminders to send today"**. Tap it.
2. For each person tap **Send on WhatsApp**. WhatsApp opens with the message already written. Press send in WhatsApp, then come back.
3. If someone already paid, tap **Paid** instead of sending.
4. Not sending to someone today? Tap **Skip**.

Tapped *Send* by mistake? Tap **Undo** on the message at the bottom, or go to **Dues → History → Back to queue**.

Reminders are prepared automatically every day at 9 AM:
3 days before the due date, on the due date, then every 3 days while unpaid (max 3 times). Change this in **Settings → Reminder rules**.

## When someone pays
1. **Members** → search their name or phone → open them.
2. Tap **Record payment** → check the amount and months → **Save payment**.
3. Optional: tap **Send receipt** to send a WhatsApp thank-you with the next due date.

The next due date moves forward by itself. Paid late? The date still continues from when they were due, so nobody gets free days.

Wrong payment? On the member's **Payments** tab tap **Undo** on the latest one.

## When someone new joins
**Members → Add member**: name, WhatsApp number, plan (fills in the fee), photo (tap *Take photo*).
Leave **Collect first fee now** on if they paid today; add an admission fee if you charge one.

## When someone stops coming
Open the member → ⋮ menu:
- **Pause membership**: on a break; no reminders, not counted as overdue.
- **Mark as left**: stopped; history is kept.
- **Delete**: only for mistakes (removes all their payments too).

## Knowing how the gym is doing
- **Dashboard**: money collected this month, what's still to collect, who's overdue, and who owes the most.
- **Analytics**: revenue by month, by payment method and plan, new members. **CSV** buttons download spreadsheets.
- **Payments**: every fee with totals; **Export CSV** for your accountant.

## Settings worth knowing
- **WhatsApp messages**: change the wording (English, Roman Urdu or Urdu). The preview shows exactly what members will get.
- **Plans**: add or change prices. Changing a price does not change existing members' fees; edit those members if needed.
- **Your data**: import members from a spreadsheet, or download all members/payments as a backup copy.

## Good habits
- Clear the reminder list every morning.
- Record payments the moment you receive them.
- Download **All members** and **All payments** once a month and keep them safe (an automatic encrypted backup also runs weekly).
- Never share your password; staff should get their own login.
